The task

The red line above is every monthly mean of carbon dioxide measured at Mauna Loa, Hawaii, from 315.6 ppm in 1959 to 427.5 ppm at the end of 2025. Your job is to find out whether it is only rising or rising faster, how strongly the planet breathes each year, and how many tonnes of carbon one ppm represents.

You will use two tools you will need for the rest of your studies. In Google Antigravity an AI agent writes the analysis code, and you review and test it. In Google Colab you run that code on the real data and share the result.
